HERITAGE & SAVOIR-FAIRE

Founded in London, Alexander McQueen represents a synthesis between rigorous Savile Row tailoring structure and radical natural romanticism.

1992 — LONDON ORIGINS

SAVILE ROW TAILORING FOUNDATION

The foundation of every McQueen collection rests upon master tailoring techniques learned on Savile Row: hand-stitched floating horsehair canvas, sharp shoulder pads, and precision lapel roll.

We treat tailoring not as decoration, but as architectural sculpture designed to transform the human silhouette.

FLORENCE & PARIS ATELIERS

ITALIAN LEATHER & FRENCH COUTURE

Our leather goods are hand-formed in Tuscany by senior artisans who have preserved moulding techniques for over four decades.

Every Wishbone handle and Manta curve undergoes rigorous tension testing to combine structural strength with featherlight ease.
