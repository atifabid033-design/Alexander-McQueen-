FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How is my Alexander McQueen order packaged?

Every online order is delivered in signature Alexander McQueen rigid gift packaging, wrapped in acid-free tissue paper and secured with grosgrain silk ribbon. A complimentary dust bag and Certificate of Authenticity are included with all leather goods and footwear.

What are the global delivery timelines & fees?

We offer complimentary Express Worldwide Delivery via DHL / FedEx carbon-neutral service on all orders. Delivery times: North America & Europe (1–3 business days), Asia-Pacific & Middle East (2–4 business days).

What is the House Return Policy?

We accept returns of unworn, unwashed merchandise with all original security tags attached within 30 days of delivery. Complimentary courier pick-up can be scheduled directly through our Return Portal.

How do I verify the authenticity of my product?

Genuine Alexander McQueen products are sold exclusively through our official website and global flagship boutiques. Each leather item features an internal serial number hot-stamped into the lining or leather lining tab.

Can I request bespoke alterations or tailoring?

Yes. Master tailoring fittings are offered exclusively at our flagship ateliers in London, Paris, New York, Tokyo, and Milan. You can schedule a private fitting appointment through our Appointment Booking Service.
