GARMENT & LEATHER LONGEVITY

PRODUCT AFTERCARE & SAVOIR-FAIRE

Our creations are crafted to endure for generations. Discover our recommended house protocols for preserving fine leather goods, Savile Row tailoring, and silk crepe gowns.

MANTA & WISHBONE BAGS

FINE CALFSKIN CARE

  • Store in the provided breathable cotton dust bag when not in use.
  • Fill with acid-free tissue paper to preserve structural leather arcs.
  • Avoid prolonged contact with dark denim or liquid perfume.
  • Clean surface gently using a dry, lint-free microfiber cloth.
SAVILE ROW TAILORING

VIRGIN WOOL PRESERVATION

  • Hang jackets on wide contoured wooden hangers to retain shoulder padding.
  • Allow 24 hours of rest between wears for natural wool fiber recovery.
  • Steam gently to release creases; avoid direct iron pressure on seams.
  • Entrust dry cleaning only to specialist luxury garment care providers.
BLAKE-STITCHED FOOTWEAR

SHOEMAKING MAINTENANCE

  • Insert cedar shoetrees immediately after wearing to absorb moisture.
  • Nourish leather uppers with natural wax cream every 6 weeks.
  • Our Paris & London ateliers offer full sole resolution and heel metal replacement.
